Cash In Transit (CIT)

At Rapid Security we offer secure banking movements that protect your staff, your cash and valuables against theft. Our Cash in Transit (CIT) services save you and your staff time-consuming and risky trips to the bank whilst guarding your staff and valuables against the risk of theft or robbery. Rapid Security is highly experienced in providing CIT services to a wide range of customers including financial institutions, retail and hospitality sectors, small business and local Government.

Depending on your needs, we can provide you with:

Armed and uniformed personnel using armoured vehicles equipped with tracking systems. Low profile and discreet services completed by armed security personnel in plain clothes, using unmarked security vehicles. Benefits of using our Cash in Transit (CIT) services:

We arrange collection of your cash, cheques or valuables according to a schedule that suits you. We ensure your cash and valuables are delivered to your nominated bank within the same business day. Using tamper evident bags provided by your bank and comprehensive Insurance, we accept liability for your cash and valuables on a declared value basis from the time of collection until delivery to your nominated bank or location.
